Milo's story

Milo was found last winter curled up beneath a park bench on a freezing December night, underweight and with an injured paw. Our team warmed him up and treated his paw. At first he hid in the back of his kennel, until someone gave him a soft blanket of his own. Little by little, he came forward. This Christmas, Milo is sleeping by the fire in his new home, and his blanket went with him.
